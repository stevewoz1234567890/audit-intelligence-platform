Standards & Frameworks

Every framework in the library, each with its own section, sidebar and structured audit content — requirements, focus areas, risks, controls, procedures, testing, evidence, red flags, findings, root causes and recommendations.

Two content tiers, by design. Openly licensed sources — FATF, Basel/BIS, INTOSAI and public regulator material — are held in full text. Copyrighted frameworks — IIA, COSO, ISACA, ACFE, IFRS, PMI, ISO — are held as complete structured references plus original practical audit guidance written for this platform. Verbatim copyrighted text is never reproduced, and every record links to its official source.
