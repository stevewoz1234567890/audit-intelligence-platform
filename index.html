Where Auditors Start

Section-scoped

Each module carries its own search and its own contextual assistance, restricted to that module's content. There is no platform-wide search and no standalone assistant — an auditor working in Payroll sees Payroll content only.

Search and AI stay inside the module. Opening IIA searches IIA only; opening an Audit Program searches that program only. Nothing queries the internet, and contextual assistance draws solely on the content of the section the auditor is working in.

Two content tiers, by design. Openly licensed sources — FATF, Basel/BIS, INTOSAI and public regulator material — are held in full text. Copyrighted frameworks — IIA, COSO, ISACA, ACFE, IFRS, PMI, ISO — are held as complete structured references plus original practical audit guidance written for this platform: risks, controls, procedures, testing, evidence, red flags, findings, root causes and recommendations. Verbatim copyrighted text is never reproduced, and every record links to the official source.
